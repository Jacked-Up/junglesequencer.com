Drop sticky notes anywhere to jot down ideas, reminders, or comments.
Sticky notes are an excellent tool to help you and your team stay organized!

This guide will walk you through using sticky notes and how to use their features.

Sticky notes can be used to annotate your graphs, making it easier to keep track of different nodes and their purposes.
You can add, move, resize, edit, change themes, and lock them to suit your needs.

![Sticky Notes](https://cdn.junglesequencer.com/v1.1.0/guides/jungle-editor/sticky-notes.gif)

---
## Creating Sticky Notes

- Via the context menu: **Right Click > Add Sticky Note**
- Or use the shortcut: **L-Shift + S**

![Creating Sticky Note Via Context Menu](https://cdn.junglesequencer.com/v2.0.0/guides/jungle-editor/creating-sticky-note-via-context-menu.gif)

---
### Grouping Nodes with Notes

Sticky notes can be used to group nodes together.
If you add a note while selecting multiple nodes, the note will surround all the selected nodes, creating a visual group.

![Grouping nodes with Sticky Notes](https://cdn.junglesequencer.com/v1.1.0/guides/jungle-editor/sticky-note-grouping-nodes.gif)

Grouping nodes with sticky notes helps in keeping related nodes together,
making your graph more organized and easier to navigate.

---
## Resizing & Moving

Sticky notes can be easily resized and moved in the graph.
- To move, click, and drag it to the desired position, just like a node.
- To resize, click and drag either the corners or sides.

![Resizing & Moving](https://cdn.junglesequencer.com/v2.0.0/guides/jungle-editor/resizing-and-moving-sticky-notes.gif)

---
## Editing Content

To edit the text of a sticky note, simply double-click on it.
This allows you to add or change the text content to reflect the information you need.

![Editing Content](https://cdn.junglesequencer.com/v2.0.0/guides/jungle-editor/editing-content-in-sticky-notes.gif)

---
## Changing Theme

You can personalize your sticky notes by changing their colors. To change the color:
1. Right-click on the sticky note
2. Hover over **Theme**
3. Select the color you prefer

![Changing Theme](https://cdn.junglesequencer.com/v2.0.0/guides/jungle-editor/changing-theme-on-sticky-notes.gif)

You can choose from yellow, green, blue, pink, and grey.

---
## Locking and Unlocking

To prevent accidental changes to a sticky note, you can lock it in place. To lock a sticky note:
1. Right-click on the sticky note
2. Select **Lock**

![Locking/Unlocking Sticky Note](https://cdn.junglesequencer.com/v1.1.0/guides/jungle-editor/sticky-note-locking-unlocking.gif)

This action will prevent you from moving, resizing, or editing the note until you unlock it.
