Hey all 👋,

I wanted to drop this post to follow up on the upcoming big update coming to Jungle Sequencer.
This one matters a ton because it pushes the project toward the quality expected in a production-ready product.
Jungle Sequencer already does its job and gives you a framework that makes development life easier!

But the code? Sometimes a little... fragile. Why?
Because it was written years ago by a **much** younger me who didn't understand good code practices.
Revamping everything was always on the roadmap. This update is that revamp. 🚀

Every. Single. File. Got. Touched.

Refactors, rewrites, improved XML documentation: all of it!
That means future updates get way easier on my end, performance is better, and yeah: Lots of bugs were squashed 🐞!

Jungle Sequencer is my passion project,
which is why I care so about it feeling as ✨high quality✨ as possible.
This update **will** deliver on that.

---
## What took so long? 😅

Short version: I'm a solo-developer who let feature creep win, and I'm glad I did.

If I had tried to "just ship smaller chunks," I don't think I could've cleaned up the old codebase.
Now the codebase is actually *pleasant* to work in, allowing me to now ship in chunks *because* of this rewrite.

- Reworking and improving **20,000+ lines** across **100+ scripts**.
- Testing everything and making sure v1.0.x versions are backwards compatible with v2.0.0.
- Completely rewriting the documentation and guides.

And yup: I did all of this myself, while being a full-time student.
Not complaining. I genuinely love this stuff. ❤️
It’s been a lot of work, but it never felt like a slog because this is what I do for fun, apparently!

---

I can’t wait to ship this and see what you build with it.
I’ve been head-down on it for so long, and I’m itching to watch it get used in the wild.
